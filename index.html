ABOUT HIM

Since childhood, he dreamt of becoming several things. A cricketer, a footballer, an astronaut, and stand-up comedian. But life always takes turns, some of which are drastic.
In June 2022, he took off to start a new journey at a residential school all the way in Tamil Nadu. He stayed away from his hometown, from his parents, from all the things he loved for 6 years (will be 6 years in 2028), to grow, to learn and to transform.

HIS FAMILY

Behind the work and milestones, there is a clear personal foundation. In Shreiyaan's life, his family plays a direct and vital role.
His parents, Parin and Bijal Shah, have consistently encouraged his ambitions, providing the trust and direction needed to build new things.

EDUCATION

He studied the ICSE curriculum at Greenlawns High School in Mumbai after which he pursued the CBSE curriculum at Chinmaya International Residential School, scoring a 95% in his 10th grade CBSE Board Examinations.
He is now undergoing the IBDP curriculum where he is diving deeper into Physics, Mathematics, Computer Science and Economics.
